  - paragraph: WELCOME TO IMTECAD 2026
  - paragraph: The Inter-regional Meeting in Tourism between the Côte d’Azur and Danang (IMTECAD) 2026 is an international event dedicated to fostering cooperation in tourism development between the Côte d’Azur (France) and Da Nang (Vietnam). The conference aims to promote tourism, strengthen cultural exchange, and build sustainable partnerships between the two regions. IMTECAD 2026 serves as a dynamic platform for tourism stakeholders, researchers, policymakers, and industry professionals to exchange ideas, explore innovative practices, and establish long-term collaborations in the tourism sector.
  - paragraph: "Date: June 16–17, 2026"
  - paragraph: "Venue: Nice, France"
  - paragraph: Partners
  - paragraph: IMTECAD 2026 is co-organized by the Danang Tourism Association, the Danang Hotel Association, the ULYSSEUS European University Alliance, Université Côte d’Azur, and Furama Resort Danang.
  - paragraph: The Danang Institute for International Tourism (DNIIT) plays a key bridging role, connecting stakeholders and coordinating the organization of the conference.
  - paragraph: "The program of IMTECAD 2026 will focus on two main themes:"
  - paragraph: "Luxury Tourism (one day):"
  - paragraph: The morning session will feature 2–3 keynote presentations followed by a round-table discussion. In the afternoon, participants will visit a tourism establishment, with opportunities to initiate and sign cooperation agreements between professionals. This theme reflects the growing interest of the Danang Tourism Association in developing luxury tourism packages to the Côte d’Azur for high-end travelers from Southeast Asia.
  - paragraph: "Perfumes and Aromas in Tourism (one day):"
  - paragraph: The morning session will include 2–3 presentations and a round-table discussion. In the afternoon, participants will visit a perfume production facility, offering opportunities for professional networking and partnership agreements. This initiative aligns with Furama Resort Danang’s vision to develop a signature perfume line for its hospitality services.
  - paragraph: IMTECAD 2026 is expected to mark a significant step forward in strengthening international cooperation in tourism, contributing to innovation, cultural exchange, and sustainable development between Europe and Southeast Asia.
